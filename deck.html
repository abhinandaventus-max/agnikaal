01 · The problem

Splice the sensors and history breaks.

  • MODIS (2000 →) sees 1 km pixels and is near end of life.
  • VIIRS (2012 →) sees 375 m pixels, so one fire lights up about 7 pixels.
  • Raw counts over India jump 5.4× at the 2012 seam: a fake fire explosion.
  • Fire managers can't compare this season with history.

02 · The harmonization engine

Four steps until the sensor of origin no longer matters.

  1. Quality filterconf ≥ 30 / nominal-high; vegetation fires only (type 0, no flares or volcanoes)
  2. Common 1 km latticeevery detection snapped to 0.01° and de-duplicated per day → Fire Cell-Days
  3. Adaptive overlap calibrationmonth-specific k̂ₘ from 14 overlap years, for the exact area drawn; empirical-Bayes shrinkage
  4. Validate + bridgeleave-one-year-out CV live in the browser; NOAA-20 ↔ S-NPP bridge into the 7-day feed
Hy,m = FCDVIIRS (y ≥ 2012)  |  k̂m·FCDMODIS (y < 2012)    k̂m = (ΣVm + λk̄)/(ΣMm + λ)

Data: NASA FIRMS MODIS C6.1 (2000–2025), VIIRS S-NPP C2 (2012–2025), NOAA-20 C2 (2018–2025), NOAA-20/21 NRT · 69.8 M detections across 5 countries processed
